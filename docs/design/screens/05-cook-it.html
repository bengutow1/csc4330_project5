Creamy Tomato Pasta

20 min Easy Serves 2
+100 pts when you plate it

Ingredients

You have 5 of 5
  • Pasta 8 oz
  • Tomatoes 3
  • Milk 1/2 cup
  • Cheddar cheese 1/2 cup
  • Olive oil 2 tbsp

Plus staples: salt, pepper, garlic powder

Steps

4 steps
  1. 1 Boil the pasta in salted water for 10 minutes.
  2. 2 Warm the olive oil and cook the chopped tomatoes for 5 minutes.
  3. 3 Stir in the milk and cheddar until the sauce is creamy.
  4. 4 Toss the pasta in the sauce, season, and serve.
